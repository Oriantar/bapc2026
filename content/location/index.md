## To Leiden by car
- On the A44 between Oegstgeest and Wassenaar take the exit 8 to Leiden,
- When driving on the Plesmanlaan at the first intersection go left on the Ehrenfestweg. 
- You can park your car on the left at the [Parkeergarage Ehrenfestweg](https://www.p1.nl/parkeerplaats/parkeergarage-ehrenfestweg/).

## To Leiden by public transport

When travelling to Leiden with the public transport you have to ride the train to _Leiden Centraal_. 

From there you take one of these busses to the busstop **Verbeekstraat**:
- 43, to "_Den Haag Centraal via Wassenaar_".
- 401, to "_Katwijk_".
- 1, to "_De Vink via Kloosterhof_".
- 22, to "_Nieuw-Vennep via Noordwijk_".
- 400, to "_Katwijk ESA/ESTEC_".
- 2, to "_De Vink via Stevenshof_".
- 250, to "_Haarlem via Lisse_".

From this busstop it's a 3 minute walk to the Gorleaus building. 

##### Bus tickets can be bought in the bus with debit card only, or you can check in using OVpay.

Planning your trip is possible from the website of the [Dutch Railways](https://www.ns.nl/en/travel-information). Train tickets can be bought at the machines at the station, or you can check in using [OVPay](https://www.ovpay.nl/en/how-it-works) using a foreign contactless debit card, a contactless credit card issued by MasterCard/Maestro and VISA/Vpay, or by phone using Google Pay or Apple Pay.
